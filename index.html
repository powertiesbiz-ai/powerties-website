Frequently asked questions

How much does an AI consultant cost?

Most AI consultants charge $150 to $500 an hour, or $2,000 to $10,000+ for a project engagement. PowerTies Consulting Group works differently: you can start with a $99 one-hour AI working session. In that hour, we map where AI can actually pay off in your business, and right after the call you get a written report — which AI tools fit, why each one, what they cost, and what they'll save you in time and money.

What does a $99 AI session include?

One focused hour with Keith Tully, founder of PowerTies Consulting Group, working on your business — not a generic lesson. You walk through your goals, your bottlenecks, and where you're losing time or money. Right after the call, you receive a written report: the AI tools that fit your situation, why each one was chosen, what each costs, and a directional estimate of the time and money each one can save you.

What happens after the $99 session?

You get the written report immediately after the call, so you can act on it right away. If you want the full executable plan, there's a $650 two-hour working session (Stage 2) that turns the Stage 1 findings into a sequenced fix list: workflow mapping from your real numbers, before-and-after process redesigns, and the biggest wins first.

Is this an AI course or a generic AI lesson?

No. It's a working session on your business. There's no slide deck of generic AI tips and no app we run for analysis — the analysis comes from the conversation about how your business actually runs. You leave with a plan built for your company, not a certificate.

Who is the $99 AI session for?

Small business owners and founder-led companies — the sweet spot is businesses doing roughly $500K or more in annual revenue, though that's flexible. What matters most is that you're willing to invest $99 and an hour to find out where AI pays off for you. If you're just curious about AI in general, start with the free scorecard below instead.

Do I need to be technical to use AI in my business?

No. Most of the AI tools worth using in a small business require no coding and no technical background — they're web apps you log into, like anything else. Part of the session is matching tools to your comfort level, so you only get recommendations you can actually run.

How quickly will I see results from AI?

Honest answer: some wins land in the first week — automating follow-up emails, speeding up quotes and proposals, cutting the time you spend on paperwork. Bigger payoffs, like reworking a whole process, take a few weeks of steady use. The session report ranks opportunities by impact per dollar, so you start with the fastest paybacks.

Will AI replace my employees?

In a small business, almost never. The realistic outcome is that your current people get more done — AI takes the repetitive drafting, summarizing, and follow-up work so your team spends its hours on customers and revenue. The session looks for where your people are drowning in busywork, not for jobs to eliminate.

What is MLOT — Money Left on the Table?

MLOT is PowerTies' free diagnostic scorecard for small businesses. It finds the leaks — the follow-ups that never happened, the quotes that went out and died, the manual work eating your week — and shows you where money is being left on the table. It takes a few minutes and it's free: mlot.vercel.app.

Is there a free way to start before paying $99?

Yes — two, in fact. Run the free MLOT scorecard at mlot.vercel.app, or take one of the short quizzes: the AI readiness quiz tells you where your business stands with AI, and the mini-MLOT quiz spots your biggest money leaks. Both are free and take about two minutes.

Does PowerTies work with my industry?

PowerTies works with small businesses across all industries — there's no single trade or sector we concentrate on. If you run a founder-led company with real operational headaches, the session format works for you. Our veteran leadership placements follow the same principle: veterans are matched with businesses of every kind, based on who the veteran is and why they're the right fit for that business.
