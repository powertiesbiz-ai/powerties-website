About Keith

Find the truth. Build what lasts.

PowerTies Consulting Group is built around three commitments: find the truth, grow what you've been given, and build a legacy worth leaving. Keith Tully is the founder and CEO, leading an execution-first consulting firm built for founder-led businesses who want an honest diagnosis, not a sales pitch.

A West Point graduate and U.S. Army veteran, Keith brings a background that spans leadership development (Thayer Leadership Development Group), financial services (Northwestern Mutual), and property and operations management, before founding PowerTies to help business owners see what's actually happening in their companies and build something that can outlast them.

He is an Elder leader at the Guild of Honour, a powerhouse networking group and business incubator built on three pillars: Honor, Sovereignty of Mind, and a Soul of Gratitude and Service.
