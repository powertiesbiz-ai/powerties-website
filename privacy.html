Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

PowerTies Consulting Group LLC (“PowerTies,” “we,” “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Contact information you provide — name, email, phone number, company name, and anything you include when you reach out, request a time, or fill in a form.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by Square. We do not see or store your full card number.
  • Booking information — when you schedule time, Calendly collects the details needed to hold the appointment.
  • Basic site data — standard, anonymized information about site usage (pages visited, device type), used only to keep the site working well.

How we use it

To deliver our services, respond to you, process payments, schedule sessions, and send the occasional letter if you've subscribed. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we share it with

Only the service providers needed to run the business — payment processing (Square), scheduling (Calendly), customer management (Zoho), and email delivery. They receive only what's necessary.

Your choices

Contact us at powertiesbiz@gmail.com any time to ask what we hold, correct it, or have it deleted.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above.
