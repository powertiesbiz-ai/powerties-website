Free tool

What would your VA disability rating actually pay?

VA ratings don’t add up the way you’d expect — a 50% rating and a 30% rating combine to 70%, not 80%. The free calculator from VetCalc works out your combined rating and estimated monthly pay with the VA’s own math and current rates, dependents included. Read it as what a rating pays if it’s granted — not a prediction of what the VA will award. Free, and no sign-up.

Open the VetCalc calculator →

The calculator is built and run by VetCalc — it is not our build, and the link opens their site. We point to it because the math is right and they keep the pay rates current.
